## Notes per product

**ChatGPT Study Mode (OpenAI).** Launched July 29, 2025 for Free/Plus/Pro/Team, Edu later; asks questions, gives hints and checks understanding rather than answering, with no admin lock-in for schools (https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/29/openai-launches-study-mode-in-chatgpt). OpenAI described it as built with teachers and pedagogy experts; the launch post could not be fetched (403) to confirm the "custom system instructions" framing. In April 2026 it vanished from the Tools menu without announcement, prompting removal reports (https://www.thirty3labs.co.uk/news/openai-removes-study-mode-chatgpt-without-announcement); later coverage says it stayed live at chatgpt.com/studymode and via @study, with a back-to-school relaunch Aug 4, 2026 (https://findskill.ai/blog/chatgpt-study-mode-back-to-school-2026/). No OpenAI learning-outcome data exists.

**ChatGPT Edu, for Teachers, Education for Countries, edu plugins (OpenAI).** ChatGPT for Teachers launched Nov 19, 2025, free for verified US K-12 educators through June 2027 (one source says 2028) (https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/19/openai-chatgpt-for-teachers-k-12-educators.html); now 100+ K-12 organizations (https://openai.com/index/bringing-chatgpt-for-teachers-to-more-us-school-districts/). Education for Countries (Jan 21, 2026) has 8 partners and a Tartu–Stanford longitudinal study of 20,000 students (https://openai.com/index/edu-for-countries/). On Aug 4, 2026 OpenAI added K-12 Educator, College Educator and College Student plugins to ChatGPT Work/Edu and Codex; the student one offers guided tutoring, quizzes and flashcards from course files (https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/04/openai-wants-teachers-and-profs-to-foist-their-work-off-on-chatgpt/5283064). Edu pricing is unpublished.

**Claude Learning Mode / Claude for Education (Anthropic).** Announced Apr 2, 2025 with Northeastern, LSE and Champlain; Learning Mode guides reasoning via Socratic questions and evidence requests (https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-for-education). Extended to all claude.ai users as a "Learning" style on Aug 14, 2025 (https://www.engadget.com/ai/anthropic-brings-claudes-learning-mode-to-regular-users-and-devs-170018471.html). Nine partner universities are listed; no outcome claims (https://claude.com/solutions/education). Anthropic's Education Reports (Apr and Aug 2025) are usage taxonomies, not pedagogy evaluations (https://anthropic.com/news/anthropic-education-report-how-university-students-use-claude).

**Claude Code Learning/Explanatory output styles (Anthropic).** Shipped Aug 14, 2025 via /output-styles: Learning mode stops and leaves #TODO blocks for the developer to write 5–10 lines; Explanatory narrates architecture tradeoffs. Framed as an answer to "brain rot" from copy-paste coding; no evaluation published (same Engadget URL).

**Claude for Teachers (Anthropic).** July 14, 2026: a free year of premium Claude (Cowork, Code, skills library, standards-mapped curricula, Learning Commons connector) for verified US K-12 staff; excludes students, no training on data, district product "coming soon" (https://www.shipmangoodwin.com/insights/anthropic-launches-free-claude-for-teachers-ai-tool-what-connecticut-school-districts-should-know.html).

**Gemini Guided Learning / LearnLM (Google).** LearnLM was folded into Gemini 2.5 on May 20, 2025 (https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/google-gemini-learnlm-update/); Guided Learning launched Aug 6, 2025 for consumer and Workspace for Education accounts, with step-by-step breakdowns, embedded diagrams/videos and flashcards from quiz results (https://9to5google.com/2025/08/06/gemini-guided-learning/). Google's own arena report had 189 educators role-play learners and 206 pedagogy experts judge; Gemini 2.5 Pro was preferred in 73.2% of non-tied match-ups vs Claude 3.7 Sonnet, GPT-4o and o3 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24477); the Dec 2024 report claimed +31% vs GPT-4o on overall pedagogy (https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16429). Strongest outcome evidence of any lab: an 8-week RCT with Fab AI in Sierra Leone (1,763 students, 12 schools) found +0.258 SD in math, with solution-seeking queries falling from 25% to 10% by week 8; gains skewed to stronger students (https://deepmind.google/blog/measuring-the-impact-of-learning-with-ai-in-sierra-leone-and-beyond/).

**Gemini for Education / Classroom (Google).** Free school-managed Gemini, teacher tools, and assignment-context study aids; in Aug 2026 Google opened Gemini study tools to students of all ages with admin controls (reported Oct 1, 2026, https://winbuzzer.com/2026/10/01/google-opens-classrooms-gemini-study-tools-to-younger-students-xcxwbn/). Google's May 19, 2026 impact post adds a 9,000-student Italy case study and an African Union partnership (https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/measuring-the-impact-of-ai-on-teaching-and-learning/). Students in several countries got 12 months of AI Pro free.

**NotebookLM study features (Google).** Sept 8, 2025: Learning Guide conversational style (probing, open-ended questions), source-grounded flashcards and quizzes with adjustable difficulty, Study Guide reports, Brief/Critique/Debate audio overviews, OpenStax textbooks (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/notebooklm-student-features/). No efficacy data.

**Copilot Learn Live and Study and Learn (Microsoft).** Oct 23, 2025 Fall Release added Learn Live, a voice Socratic tutor with whiteboard and the Mico avatar, US consumer Copilot first (https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/copilot/articles/forget-clippy-meet-mico-microsofts-194007109.html). Oct 15, 2025: Study and Learn agent (flashcards, matching, quizzes, "grounded in learning science") and Teach, free for education tenants; M365 Copilot academic SKU $18/user/month from Dec 2025 for ages 13+ (https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/education/blog/2025/10/designing-microsoft-365-copilot-to-empower-educators-students-and-staff/). Only time-saved surveys cited; underlying model unstated.

**Learning Accelerators and Khanmigo (Microsoft).** Reading Coach gives real-time pronunciation and fluency feedback with AI-generated stories; included in free Office 365 A1 and as a standalone app; cites Digital Promise research-based design certification rather than outcome studies (https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/education/learning-tools/learning-accelerators). Khanmigo for Teachers has been free via the Microsoft partnership since Aug 2024 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khan_Academy); the Phi-3 math fine-tune details could not be verified (page 404).

**Grok (xAI).** No dedicated study mode found. Dec 11–12, 2025: El Salvador deal to deploy curriculum-adapted Grok to 5,000+ public schools and 1M+ students over two years, with audit protocols promised but no evaluation plan (https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2025/12/12/latam-elsalvador-xai-elon-musk-grok-tutoring-public-schools/2821765570393/). A 2-month free SuperGrok .edu promo ran and appears lapsed (https://krater.ai/blog/grok-student-discount).

**Perplexity Study Mode / Learn Mode.** Study Mode (Sept 2025) explains concepts step-by-step at the user's level and generates flashcards and quizzes; Learn Mode (Feb 2026) breaks topics into lesson modules with self-tests, toggle limited to verified students (https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/12120542-what-is-learn-mode, https://www.perplexity.ai/changelog/what-we-shipped-september-5th). Both official pages blocked the fetch; details rest on search snippets. Education Pro about $10/month via SheerID after the free .edu promo ended Jan 2026 (https://aiinsightsnews.net/is-perplexity-pro-free-for-students-in-2026-what-still-works/).

**Mistral Vibe Education plan.** Le Chat became Vibe in May 2026 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Chat_(AI)); Student plan $5.99/month for 12 months vs Pro $14.99 (https://mistral.ai/pricing). No tutoring mode; adoption via business schools (NEOMA, TBS, IMT) (https://www.tbs-education.fr/inspiring/tbs-education-deploie-2-000-licences-mistral/).

**Meta.** ai.meta.com/learn is a how-to hub, not a tutor (https://ai.meta.com/learn/). Meta's education footprint is Llama powering third parties such as Blended Labs' Lumi and FoondaMate (https://ai.meta.com/blog/blended-labs-ai-driven-schools-with-llama/).

**Chinese labs.** Doubao Aixue (Doubao model, Volcano OCR) does photo-solve, AI grading, essay tutoring and paid 1-on-1; promoted to a core Doubao entry point Jan 2026; 19.4M MAU by June 2026 (https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Doubao%20Aixue/1478557). Alibaba's Qwen Zhixue and a gaokao-application agent, Baidu's Wenxin gaokao module and Tencent Yuanbao all pushed exam-season features in June–July 2026, with media finding outdated data (https://restofworld.org/2026/china-ai-bot-college-advice/). China's AI-education apps passed 120M MAU with no demonstrated outcome link (https://hellochinatech.com/p/china-ai-education-120-million-users-zero-revenue). DeepSeek: no tutoring product found; unverified. Gauth (ByteDance) not verified.

## Notable trends and gaps

- Every major Western lab now ships a "don't just answer" mode (Study Mode, Learning Mode, Guided Learning, Learn Live, Learn Mode), all launched July–Oct 2025 within weeks of each other; mechanics converge on Socratic questions, hints, quizzes and flashcards.
- Only Google publishes pedagogy evaluations of its own tutor: two LearnLM technical reports (expert preference arenas) and one RCT. No pedagogy rubric, leaderboard or outcome study from OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, xAI, Perplexity, Mistral, Meta or the Chinese labs.
- The lab-published evals are self-run and compare against rivals' older models (e.g., Claude 3.7 vs Gemini 2.5 Pro); no cross-lab shared protocol exists.
- Independent benchmarks are emerging instead: Scale's TutorBench (Oct 3, 2025; 1,490 multimodal prompts, 15,220 rubric criteria; best model about 56–69%; Claude leads on active-learning hints but trails on explanation/feedback) (https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02663, https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/tutorbench); Handshake's StudentBench (Sept 2026; 2,383-person RCT, 13 AI tutors statistically equivalent to human GRE tutors, Gemma 4 31B at 918x lower cost) (https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28470); BEA 2025 shared task (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.10579).
- Modes are opt-in and easily toggled off; no vendor offers teacher/parent enforcement at the consumer level (OpenAI explicitly declined), and OpenAI's silent April 2026 UI change showed how fragile discoverability is.
- Pedagogy is mostly prompt/system-instruction layered on a general model; only Google claims the behaviors are trained in (LearnLM fine-tuning), and no lab discloses the model behind its tutoring mode beyond Google (Gemini 2.5/3) and Perplexity (unstated).
- Commercial push is toward educators and institutions (ChatGPT for Teachers, Claude for Teachers, M365 Copilot academic SKU, Education for Countries) rather than student-facing learning gains; Anthropic has no student K-12 product at all.
- Outcome evidence is thin and mostly in LMIC math (Sierra Leone) or test-prep (GRE); nothing yet on writing, humanities, or K-12 Western classrooms, which is a clear opening for an independent benchmark.
